Sales Roleplay

Do not “perform sales.” Diagnose first. Your job is to show curiosity, structure and a clean next step.

Sell Me This Pen

Discovery-first structure

  1. Ask: “Before I pitch it, what do you normally use a pen for — quick notes, signing documents, or something else?”
  2. Probe: “What do you dislike about the one you use now?”
  3. Align: pick one relevant feature/value only.
  4. Check: “Would that solve the issue you mentioned?”
  5. Close: “Would you be open to trying this one?”
The test is not whether the pen is impressive. The test is whether you can discover before pitching.
Live Objection

“Your solution is too expensive.”

“That makes sense. When you say expensive, is the concern the absolute budget, the comparison with another option, or that the value is not clear enough yet?”

Then respond to the real objection. Do not discount immediately. Re-anchor on business impact and agree a next step.

Mini Discovery Call — Cisco Scenario

Customer says: “We’re rolling out more AI workloads next year.”

Questions you could ask

  • “Where will those workloads run — centralized data center, cloud, edge, or a mix?”
  • “What is the biggest constraint today: performance, security, visibility, cost, or time to deploy?”
  • “Who owns the business outcome, and who owns the infrastructure decision?”
  • “What happens if the current environment cannot support the scale you need?”
